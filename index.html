The breed

Why an Airedale

Airedale Terriers take their name from the Aire Valley in Yorkshire. They are smart, sturdy, and happiest when their family is close.

A real family dog

Airedales make great family dogs: loyal, playful, and glad to be wherever their people are.

A coat that stays put

The wiry coat sheds very little, and we raise our Airedales as a hypoallergenic breed. Every family's allergies differ, so meet the dogs before you decide.

Made for Idaho

Versatile indoor and outdoor dogs. They settle in on the couch and still have energy for the trail.
